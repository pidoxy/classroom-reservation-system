# Code of Conduct

This is a learning space. Everyone here is at a different point, and that is the point.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Ask a question before making a claim.
- Say what you do not know. Nobody here is expected to know everything.
- Credit others' ideas and help.
- Keep discussion on topic and respectful.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing private information about others.
- Spam or promotion unrelated to the project.

## Reporting

If something is wrong, tell a maintainer through a private message to the repository owner, or open an issue if you are comfortable doing so. Reports are handled fairly and kept as private as possible.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may remove people who repeatedly do.

This code is adapted from the ideas in the Contributor Covenant.
